Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They are used to keep you signed in, to secure the site and to make it work.

## 3. Strictly necessary cookies

Session and security cookies are required to provide the service. If you block them, you may not be able to sign in to the customer portal or submit forms.

## 4. Your choices

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies prevents sign-in to the customer portal.

Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookieless; no cookie consent banner is required for this analytics tool. It runs on public site pages only when a site token is configured, and is not loaded in the admin panel or customer portal.
