# Privacy Policy

Last updated: [YÜRÜRLÜK TARİHİ]

This policy explains how personal data is processed when you visit the website and customer portal operated by [ŞİRKET UNVANI] (the "Company", "Site"), and when you use our services. For details, see the [Data Protection Notice](kvkk-aydinlatma.md).

## 1. Information we collect

**Information you provide directly**

- Name, e-mail, phone, company, message and product/edition details entered in demo, quote, contact and support forms.
- Name, e-mail, company, phone, tax number or national ID and preferred language when you create a customer account.
- Text you submit when opening a support ticket or replying to one.
- Profile details you update in your account settings.

**Information collected automatically while you use the Site**

- IP address, date and time, requested page, browser and device information (server access logs).
- Verification results and rate-limit records for form submissions.
- A session identifier (only when you sign in to the customer portal; see the Cookie Policy).
- Cookieless, aggregated visit statistics. [DOĞRULA: whether the analytics tool is active]

**License and download records**

- Product, edition, seats, dates and status of licenses assigned to your account; access records for licensed downloads.

The Site does not collect payment card or bank details, because there is no online checkout.

## 2. How we use information

- To respond to demo, quote and information requests.
- To manage your customer account, licenses, downloads and support tickets.
- To keep your account secure, including password reset and e-mail verification.
- To prevent spam, abuse and automated attacks.
- To measure site performance and content use in aggregated, cookieless form.
- To meet legal obligations.
- To send marketing communications only if you have consented. You can opt out at any time.

## 3. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only as necessary to operate the service, with:

- Server, hosting and backup providers ([SAĞLAYICI — DOĞRULA]).
- Cloudflare Turnstile, for bot protection.
- E-mail delivery provider ([SAĞLAYICI — DOĞRULA]).
- Public authorities, where legally required.

Form notifications are sent to Company staff responsible for handling the request. Staff access is limited by role and need.

## 4. Retention

Information is kept for as long as needed for the purpose and as required by legal retention obligations. See the Data Protection Notice for details. [SAKLAMA SÜRELERİ — DOĞRULA]

Database backups are kept for [SAKLAMA SÜRESİ — DOĞRULA] and then deleted automatically.

## 5. Security

The Site uses HTTPS; session cookies are set with HttpOnly and SameSite attributes; forms are validated on the server; account actions are subject to rate limits and authorisation checks. No transmission or storage method is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Do not share your password with anyone. If you notice something suspicious, contact [KVKK İLETİŞİM E-POSTASI].

## 7. Children
