# Refund and Withdrawal Policy

Last updated: [YÜRÜRLÜK TARİHİ]

This policy explains the refund, cancellation and withdrawal terms for software licenses bought online from [ŞİRKET UNVANI] (the "SELLER"). Read it together with the [Distance Sales Agreement](mesafeli-satis-sozlesmesi.md) and the [Pre-Information Form](on-bilgilendirme-formu.md).

## 1. Nature of the product

The product is a software license delivered electronically as soon as payment is confirmed (digital content, intangible good). There is no physical delivery.

## 2. Right of withdrawal and its exception for consumers

- Under Consumer Protection Law No. 6502 and the Distance Contracts Regulation, a consumer may withdraw from a distance contract within fourteen days.
- Under Article 15 of the Regulation, the right of withdrawal cannot be exercised for contracts for services performed instantly in electronic form or intangible goods delivered instantly to the consumer, nor for contracts whose performance began with the consumer's consent before the withdrawal period expired. - Because of the separate, explicit consent you give during the order ("I consent to the License being performed immediately in electronic form and I understand that my right of withdrawal ends once performance begins") and because the License is issued right after payment, **the right of withdrawal cannot be exercised once performance has begun.** The order cannot be completed without this consent.
- Before performance begins (payment taken but the License not yet issued) you can ask for cancellation by writing to [KVKK İLETİŞİM E-POSTASI]. [HUKUK İNCELEMESİ: how this case is handled]

## 3. Corporate buyers

Corporate buyers ordering with a tax ID number are not consumers; the right of withdrawal does not apply. Refund or cancellation requests are assessed under the order terms and the agreement between the parties. [HUKUK İNCELEMESİ]

## 4. Refunds and cancellations the SELLER accepts

Even outside the right of withdrawal, a refund may be given in these cases: duplicate or erroneous charges; the License could not be delivered for a technical reason, or the product does not match the stated characteristics and the problem cannot be remedied within a reasonable time; and refunds the SELLER separately agrees in writing. ## 5. Refund process

- Write to [KVKK İLETİŞİM E-POSTASI] with your order number. You can use the withdrawal form below or free text.
- Approved refunds are made to the original payment method through the payment institution within fourteen days at the latest from when the request reaches the SELLER. The time until the money shows in your account depends on your card issuer.
- For foreign-currency payments the refund is made in the payment currency; exchange differences and bank deductions are outside the SELLER's control.
- When a refund is approved the related License is cancelled and you must stop using the software. The invoice is corrected in line with the refund. [HUKUK İNCELEMESİ: refund invoice/e-Archive handling]

## 6. Subscriptions

Subscription licenses do not renew automatically; a separate renewal order or quote is needed. No refund is given for the period after a subscription ends.

## 7. Defective performance

A consumer BUYER's statutory rights for defective goods/digital content under the Law are unaffected. You can use the same contact address for them.
