## 3. Payment

- Payment is collected through the SELLER's contracted payment institution [ÖDEME KURULUŞU — örn. iyzico]. Card details are entered on the payment institution's secure page; the SELLER does not see or store card data.
- The order is completed when the payment institution confirms the payment to the SELLER. If payment fails or cannot be verified, no order is placed and no License is issued.

- The invoice is issued within the legal period based on the billing details given in the order and delivered to the BUYER by e-mail and/or in the customer portal. [HUKUK İNCELEMESİ: e-Archive/e-Invoice and delivery method]

## 4. Delivery (performance)

- The License is delivered electronically and immediately: once the payment is confirmed, the license key is generated, assigned to the BUYER's account in the customer portal and notified by e-mail. There is no physical delivery or shipping.
- The License is issued for the product, seat count and term in the edition definition. A subscription License ends on its start date plus the license term. A subscription does not renew automatically; renewal requires a separate order or quote.
- Use of the software is also subject to the End User License Agreement (EULA): [End User License Agreement](eula.md).
- The general thirty-day maximum delivery period (Law art. 48) does not apply because performance is immediate; performance occurs when payment is confirmed.

## 5. Right of withdrawal and its exception

- **Individual (consumer) BUYER:** under the Law and the Regulation, a consumer may in principle withdraw from a distance contract within fourteen days of its conclusion without giving a reason and without penalty.
- However, under Article 15 of the Regulation, the right of withdrawal cannot be exercised for contracts for services performed instantly in electronic form or intangible goods delivered instantly to the consumer, nor for services and digital content whose performance began with the consumer's consent before the withdrawal period expired. [HUKUK İNCELEMESİ: confirm the relevant sub-paragraphs of Regulation art. 15 and the wording of the performance consent]
- The License is an intangible good delivered instantly in electronic form. Therefore a **separate, explicit, not pre-ticked consent** is taken during the order: "I consent to the License being performed immediately in electronic form and I understand that my right of withdrawal ends once performance begins." The order cannot be completed without it. The consent is recorded with its version and a timestamp.
- Once consent is given and performance has begun (after the License is issued), the right of withdrawal cannot be exercised. Details and the withdrawal form are in the [Refund and Withdrawal Policy](iade-ve-cayma.md).
- **Corporate (tax ID) BUYER:** the buyer acts for commercial or professional purposes, so the consumer provisions of the Law (including withdrawal) do not apply. Refund and cancellation requests are agreed separately. [HUKUK İNCELEMESİ: validity of these provisions for corporate buyers and how this text is shown]

## 6. Defective performance and support

If the License or software does not conform to the characteristics in the product documentation, the BUYER may contact the SELLER in writing. The SELLER will try to remedy the problem within a reasonable time; if it cannot be remedied, the BUYER's statutory rights remain reserved. A consumer BUYER's statutory remedies for defective goods under the Law are unaffected. [HUKUK İNCELEMESİ: digital content conformity provisions]

## 7. Cancellation of the License

The License of an order whose payment is refunded, cancelled or reversed by a chargeback is cancelled, and the BUYER stops using the software. Details are in the EULA and the Refund and Withdrawal Policy.
