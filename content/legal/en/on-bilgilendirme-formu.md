## 4. Delivery and performance

The License is delivered electronically right after the payment is confirmed: the license key appears in the customer portal account and is notified by e-mail. There is no physical shipment. Performance does not begin until payment is confirmed.

## 5. Right of withdrawal

- A consumer may in principle withdraw from a distance contract within fourteen days.
- **Important:** the License is an intangible good performed and delivered instantly in electronic form. Under Article 15 of the Regulation, the right of withdrawal cannot be exercised once performance has begun with the consumer's consent. A separate checkbox collects this consent: "I consent to the License being performed immediately in electronic form and I understand that my right of withdrawal ends once performance begins." The order cannot be completed without it. [HUKUK İNCELEMESİ: sub-paragraph numbers and consent wording]
- Details on the use and scope of the right of withdrawal and the withdrawal form: [Refund and Withdrawal Policy](iade-ve-cayma.md).
- Corporate buyers (with a tax ID number) are not consumers; the withdrawal provisions do not apply. [HUKUK İNCELEMESİ]
